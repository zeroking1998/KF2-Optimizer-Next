# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want a technically rigorous and welcoming project. Be respectful, patient,
and specific. Critique ideas and evidence, not people. Assume good intent while
still asking for reproducible facts.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, publishing another person’s private information, deliberate
disruption, or sustained personal attacks.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems privately to the repository owner through the security
contact configured on the repository. Maintainers may edit or remove content,
close contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
needed to protect the community.

Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, repetition, and whether
the participant takes responsibility and corrects the behavior. Reports are
handled as confidentially as practical.
