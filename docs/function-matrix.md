## Evidence-gated laboratory scope

| ID | Decision and concrete reason |
|---|---|
| L01 Gameplay telemetry | KF2's own `Launch.log` provides bounded process-bound session context and AI/wave snapshots. A pinned official-SDK-compiled schema-6 Published mutator is prepared through `LocalOptions` and inserts a separate interaction into KF2's unchanged native viewport. The interaction creates the probe only in a standalone gameplay world. The probe adds read-only freshness-limited enemy class/boss/visibility/LOD/animation/injury and exact special-move buckets; corpse physics/visibility/age/runtime-budget, exact detached-limb/ragdoll-warning and four runtime-collision decisions; five decal managers; official smoke/fire/toxic/puke-mine/explosion categories; gore/world/ground-fire/impact particle; and FleX-surrogate aggregates. Its separately policy-gated corpse actuator is not a telemetry source. The probe destroys itself outside `NM_Standalone`; general persistent per-actor telemetry and exact creation events remain unavailable, while each confirmed Optimizer distance-sleep, proximity-wake and visible-ragdoll-sleep receipt carries a session-local `corpse_id`, distance and effective-awake readback. Optional Debug-page markers show that corpse ID for ten seconds and can separately show metre distances plus session IDs for at most 64 visible living Zeds, using value-only snapshots refreshed no faster than 100 ms. |
| L02 Enemy rendering | KF2/UE3 native view-frustum and occlusion culling remains authoritative. Fresh visible-enemy pressure scales continuously from five to eighty weighted Zeds; recently rendered enemies inside 1,200/600 units add one/two extra pressure points. The protected offline controller may reversibly raise living-monster `MinLodModel` while moving its activation distance from 600 toward 300 units and adds another stage beyond 1,200 units, consuming every real mesh LOD available while always clamping to the mesh's final LOD. Exact readback, ownership-aware near restore and session restore are required; enemies are never hidden. |
| L03 Enemy animation | Under the same pressure, the controller uses KF2's documented writable `AnimationLODDistanceFactor` and `AnimationLODFrameRate` inputs (0.15/2 or 0.25/3, plus one distant rate step). The engine's `bSkipTickAnimNodes`, `bSkipGetBoneAtoms`, interpolation, required-bone, attack and hit state remain engine-owned; exact readback and restore are required. |
| L04 Corpses/ragdolls | `MaxDeadBodies`, lifetimes and shipped corpse-collision switches are reversible. The three cosmetic corpse-collision switches are staged by CPU quality at 80/60/40% with exact readback and restoration; living-enemy collision/navigation and `bAllowPhysics` remain unchanged. Protected telemetry observes active/sleep/final-pose, visibility, LOD, injury, age, capacity and official offscreen rules. Its independently reported corpse capability treats the selected 4-2000 value as the stable user ceiling. Reaching it alone performs no cleanup: a fresh current sample and sustained target-relative pressure must both be present before a separate runtime threshold drops in bounded steps and KF2 drains excess corpses one at a time. Current recovery stops removal immediately and restores capacity gradually toward the selected ceiling. Distant physics is separate and distance-first: an old slow offscreen corpse beyond 1,200 units may sleep every 400 ms without FPS pressure; visible enemy/corpse density or confirmed frame pressure moves entry to 1,000/850 units and 200/100 ms. Tracked bodies wake inside 800 units. Reversible corpse `MinLodModel` applies outside 300 units, restores inside 250 units and becomes more aggressive under enemy/scene/frame pressure; visible-ragdoll sleep starts at low-to-medium scene density. A separate 50-ms aging cursor inspects one corpse and performs at most one transition per invocation: 3/8/15-second tiers progressively reduce real LOD, freeze skeleton work only after physics is asleep, and use verified sleep. The 3/8-second tiers keep 1,200/1,000-unit distance guards; the 15-second final tier waits until a corpse is at least 800 units away and no longer recently rendered, then moves it once to `PHYS_None` after sleep readback. The persistent cursor later revisits deferred corpses. That prevents native wake and later physical hit/explosion/collision reaction without freezing a nearby visible body. Final age LOD persists without repeat writes; Adaptive-off restoration processes one tracked corpse every 50 ms. Collision channels, cleanup ownership, living Zeds, death animation and Zed Time remain protected. |
| L05 Gore/decals/particles | Verified limits/lifetimes/distance policy plus actual wound/splatter/pool/impact/explosion decal counts, gore/world/ground-fire/impact particle counts, visibility, LOD and bounds are implemented. Fresh visible particle occupancy and decal saturation drive a separate reversible overdraw quality channel with exact readback for particle distortion/LOD, blood effects and decal budgets. Spray/explosion actors expose official gameplay-capable semantics; exact creation events, per-pixel coverage and per-effect simulation cost remain unavailable and are not guessed. |
| L06 Advanced FleX | The legacy crashing proxy remains discarded. Its independently rebuilt minimal replacement has 37 exact PE forwarders plus fifteen local ABI relays, including exact set/wait fence, solver bounds, parameter-call and six particle/phase/velocity bulk-transfer observations, offline-only gating, identity-bound observations and control, transactional install/restore and automated loader soak. Transfer and parameter payloads are never read or changed. Hysteresis-stabilized Adaptive substep control covers 1-5 with warmup and stale-control fallback; fresh visible-enemy pressure may proactively lower substeps only when the user already enabled FleX. Historical gameplay observations belong to an older candidate and are not treated as proof for the current uncommitted build. The low-level API still does not identify living enemies, active corpses or inactive corpses, so the program never guesses semantic solver groups. |
| L07 Adaptive performance | The complete V2 target registry and deterministic governor exist, including locks, prediction, target-relative stability state, budgets, hysteresis, fast correction, slow recovery, watchdogs, typed proposed/pending/applied receipts, generation checks, decision history and strict promotion metadata. Verified standalone gameplay has a separate authenticated loopback actuator for bounded CPU/GPU/VRAM/RAM/overdraw/mixed native quality groups. It acts only with valid current telemetry, pauses during Zed Time and accepts a change only after exact engine readback; display, pacing, anti-aliasing and FleX stay user-owned. Unproved targets remain `SHADOW/TEST_REQUIRED`, protected gameplay/security targets remain blocked, and config-only values retain automatic snapshot/apply/readback/restore. |

## Permanent exclusions

X01 gameplay authority, X02 network authority, X03 removal/freezing of living
enemies, X04 guessed telemetry, X05 unknown-address/process-memory injection,
and X06 anti-cheat/kernel/security bypass remain prohibited. The requested FleX
laboratory is a file-bound, explicit offline compatibility experiment and may
not weaken these exclusions.
